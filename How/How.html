How's this all gonna pan out?

The Plan

First things first, what are my plans for Femboy Day 2027?

Like I said in the introduction, the rough plan is to talk among all the femboy content creators I am able to reach, and coordinate a day of *candid conversation and expression*.
To be clear, this does NOT include any organised event, like a big livestream, collab video, or anything of the sort. As much as I wish I were able to pull something like that off, it’s just not feasible without a pre-existing audience and platform, neither of which I have.


I do not want to pretend like I have some perfect guidelines to follow, preordained by a higher power. All I have are my own thoughts on how to approach the matter, and I do not want to demand that anybody follows these to the letter. Each creator knows their audience, and how they might best respond. The following guidelines are just my own amateurish reasoning on how to clearly show a non-fetishised perspective on the femboy community. I've split it into advice for SFW creators, and NSFW creators, but I need to say that those categories aren't mutually exclusive, and the lines are very blurry (especially for a lot of femboys).

For SFW Creators

SFW Creators, meaning streamers, youtubers and similar who, while they may make a dick joke, or even actively put on a sexualised performance of themselves, do not make explicitly pornographic content.

More or less, the main idea I hope to convey to SFW content creators is candidness.
Candidness, as in having genuine conversations about what it's like being a femboy, without caving to the popular perception that it's all about being "submissive and breedable" or whatever else. However, I need to stress that I don't want to advocate for some sort of purity culture, or complete sterility. Make lewd jokes, and be rude, just be careful not to generalise as is so often done. Slut shaming is incredibly toxic, and would destroy any credibility I hope this movement can have.
Also, to be clear, I don't want this to be depressing. of course, speak plainly, but if we are all just spending the day complaining and whining about what ails us, there's nothing gained. Celebrate the genuine diversity of the femboy community. Be happy. Be gay - in any sense of the word.

For NSFW Creators

NSFW Creators, meaning sex workers, porn creators, and the like.

In my opinion, and this is something I hope to write more fully about in the -Further Reading- section, the porn creators and other sex workers are the femboy community’s best bet for desexualising ourself. That seems counter-intuitive at first, but as it stands, the porn creation side of the femboy community is the side with the most attention and publicity. If we want to be heard, we need to use what voice we already have, and that voice mostly lies with the sex workers in our community.

Things to avoid

Like I talked about previously, it's important not to make things so drab. Of course, we need to have discussions about the problems with the fetishisation of the femboy community, and all the disgusting ways it's being used to prey on us, but just as important is to celebrate the beautiful realities of the femboy community.
Avoid slut shaming. It serves to do nothing but drive a wedge between us. Whether you're a sex repulsed ace femboy, or a hypersexual sex worker, everybody has the right to express themselves how they feel most comfortable. But an equally important thing to avoid is generalisations. Half of all the memes concerning the femboy community, are about stereotyping us as hyper-sexual, submissive, seductive or any other fetishistic fantasy our chasers have about us. Without a doubt, *some* femboys are these things, just like any other group. However, the assertion that these things hold for even *most* femboys is ridiculous. BE CAREFUL when making jokes and making memes, because the whole point of this event is to drive home the fact that femboys are not inherently and homogeniously sexual.
