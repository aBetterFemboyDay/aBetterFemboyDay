What is Femboy Day?

Femboy day gained slight popularity a few years ago, intending to be a celebration of femboys and gender non-conformity...
However, there's not much to it at the moment...
Therefore, I think it's about time we, the femboy community, made use of it for something worthwhile.


Recently I've been getting increasingly more annoyed at how fetishised the femboy community is. How most people who aren't femboys (and also a lot who are) consistently use language that focuses on femboys as seductive, or as objects of desire. And what's worse, how nobody seems to be doing anything about it.

Disclaimer

There will eventually be mentions of sensitive topics, including sex, and sexual abuse. I will try to corral these as best as possible, but it's such an important part of the discussion that avoiding it in full is futile. If you are sensitive to these topics, then read with caution.
In addition, I should state that, unfortunately, I have no research papers or actual facts and figures to refer to, so be warned that everything I claim should be taken with a grain of salt. It's all anecdotes and conjecture.

Plans for Femboy Day 2026

My hope for femboy day this year, is to turn the volume down a little on everything that's sexual within the femboy community, so we can let the real and candid sides of the community thrive without feeling drowned out.
The obvius question then is "how do we make that happen?" ...and that's also where I'm sort of struggling.
As of right now, I think the only step to take, is to just ask everybody. If we can all come together for a day, for the sake of the community, then we may be able to genuinely show what being a femboy is ACTUALLY about.


Beyond that, I don't rightfully know, but I hope that this can be a starting point for further discussion. If there's anybody who has any further ideas, or better ideas, please try to reach me through my email. I really want to help repair the reputation of the femboy community in whatever way I can.
